If you have been wondering how this weird connection came about between me listening to a lot of very complex, almost chaotic metal and similarly complex, but not nearly as chaotic fusion, the answer is Plini. Plini (but, for that matter, also artists such as Sarah Longfield, David Maxim Micic, or Sithu Aye) sits right in Between the Buried and Me and fusion bands such as Snarky Puppy or Mark Lettieri. In fact, another of my (fusion) recommendations in this list, sungazer, once served as an opening act for a Plini tour in Australia.

Plini’s style is very bright, almost hopeful, but still energetic and as complex as all the other artists in this scene. Again, my favorite song is the opener of the record, Electric Sunrise, which sounds just about as you would expect from its title. It starts low and slow, but steadily grows until its zenith with a wall of sound that certainly wakes you up. This record also gets a very high rating because it’s an album that I tend to listen to multiple times in a row on repeat. Highly recommend — again, as is the case for most of this list, even for people who typically don’t feel like jumping at the label “metal.”
