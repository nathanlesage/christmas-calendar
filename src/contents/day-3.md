Another album I discovered mostly by accident as it has been flushed into a random playlist by Apple Music a few years ago in the summer. But at the same time, I actually stopped to consciously listen to the song, see which album it came from, and started listening it properly – from start to finish. What awaited me was something surprisingly good. The entire album combines three things I absolutely adore: complex technical riffs, speed and energy, and space. It starts with an almost 80s-like intro, before diving into a journey of classical synthesizers and tight guitars. Now, it is quite complex in technicality, but what I like about the record is that it never becomes mechanical. It always has emotional depth to it, and there is no complexity where complexity isn’t needed.

Satellite is actually the first album I bought on Bandcamp after my ten-year hiatus, because it’s just *so good*. Also, how can you *not* like an album with the song “rocket cat” on it? Tell me!
