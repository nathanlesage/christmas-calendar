We all know and love/hate those bands that turned out to be mere one-hit-wonders. I feel that Light Bearer is such an example, but also a tragical one. A lot of one-hit-wonders like Liquido, Aqua, or other crimes stemming from the 1990s have this one song for which they’re famous and nothing else. We listen to these songs primarily at parties but otherwise we essentially forget about them. Light Bearer is a different kind of one-hit-wonder. It starts with one of the most brutally-beautiful songs ever written, and contains five additional songs that are okay but completely forgettable. But that one song? It’s part of almost all of my playlists and gets played at least once per week. To me, personally, Beautiful is this Burden is a masterpiece of songwriting that strums a very particular chord within me. The song has been so influential that one of my own compositions accidentally ended up using the particular strumming pattern they used in this song, a fact that I only realized years after writing the song. If you now think “well, but why should I buy an entire album if only one song is really good?”, then lucky you: First, the song is over 18 minutes in length, and second the album is (at the time of writing) downloadable for free!
