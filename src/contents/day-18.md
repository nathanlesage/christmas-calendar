Do you know this feeling when you go to a music festival, have zero idea who’s playing, so you just head to a random location and keep an open mind to whatever is coming your way? And then, a totally random stranger gets on the stage, and just rips? That’s my experience I had with Loviet. I discovered her in 2022 at the Future Echoes festival in Norrköping. She was playing a small set in the first floor of Harry’s, close to Arbetetsmuseum.

I have never heard of her before, nor did I know what to expect. But man, the performance was exceptional. The best was: Since there were not many people present, it was quite private, and I even got the chance to chat a bit with her afterwards. To me, it was crazy that she went all the way from Vancouver, Canada, to some random Swedish town for a concert, but on the other hand, she seemed to enjoy it very much, and I had a new favorite artist for the coming months. In fact, because she never has released a full-length album, only singles and EPs, when I reactivated my music library, I just went ahead and pressed “Ctrl-A” when deciding which records to get.

It almost feels as if she’s circumventing the one-hit-wonder trap by simply releasing hit after hit without cramping a bunch of forgettable songs on the same record. But even the EPs she’s released (for example, 777 or DEBUTANTE), are great experiences through and through. But again, if I had to choose, it would always be One.
