This recommendation almost feels a bit cheeky. I still vividly remember the very odd feeling I had in the vicinity of this album launch. So here’s how it went: There was this random new band from Southern California that nobody had heard of before. My best friend shot me the recommendation essentially on the day Sunbather was released, and I gave it a listen. I was immediately hooked. Black metal *with melodies?!* Hell yeah!

And I wasn’t the only one thinking that. I remember the entire music industry going wild over that release. It was an instant hit — and it was black metal, mind you, a genre known for many things but its wide appeal. A few weeks later, it got even weirder. Apple released their iPhone 5C models. Except for being in the same U.S. state, Apple and Deafheaven had nothing in common, but for whatever reason, some engineer or designer at Apple apparently got notice of Sunbather, and promptly gave the band an insane kickstart into the global world by providing one of its marketing materials (for the orange iPhone 5C case) with a screenshot that showed Sunbather playing in iTunes. I would have never thought that that could ever happen, yet here we are.

In fact, the album is still so genre-defining that the entire crowd at a concert will get a meltdown when Deafheaven starts playing Dream House. How I know? Well, because they were in Stockholm in August 2026, and guess what the last song in the encore was? Needless to say, even the typically calm and controlled Swedes completey lost it. That was a full-body experience.

So, yeah. Even if you think “Eww, I don’t like metal,” I suggest you give it a listen. *I think you’re going to love it!*
