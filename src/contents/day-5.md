Maybeshewill, maybe she won’t. We’re unlikely to ever know. But that’s fine, because what counts is the music. Maybeshewill is one of the earlier bands I’ve discovered as I journeyed into my own musical taste and developed a feel for what is good and band music. Maybeshewill do have a slightly sterile tone to them to a certain degree, but that doesn’t fully go against the feeling the music evokes. As is customary for any decent British band, the records are somewhat dark, futile, and evoke the feeling of a rainy November night. Just as Mark Fisher has predicted more than ten years ago, the UK is one of those countries in which hopelessness is prevalent. But Maybeshewill has something to counter it. Across a variety of records, all of which are excellent and recommendable, the band both takes the *zeitgeist* seriously, and tries to offer some solace – by quite literally spreading hope and trying.

One thing for which the band has become quite famous is their way of weaving in sound bites from famous movies into their songs. There’s the “Mad as Hell”-speech from the movie “Network” (1976) that is featured extensively on the title track of the record, Not for Want of Trying. Without this album, I probably wouldn’t have ever heard about this movie, whose most important acting part became so popular for a while that Instagram, YouTube, and maybe TikTok (I’m not there and proud of it) was full of little references to Network for a few short years. Now, while the title song is quite good, my personal favorite is clearly “He Films the Clouds Pt. 2,” a little later on the record. Mostly because it has a quite progression and ends in an almost stadion-rock-esque hymn towards the end.
