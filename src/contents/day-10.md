Even if you have not heard the music from 65daysofstatic knowingly, you have probably listened to at least one song from this very album, namely: Debutante. It has been used in the [2014 trailer for the game No Man’s Sky](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJEJe8HMsY4). 65daysofstatic composed the entire score for the game, which catapulted the not-so-famous British band into the global spotlight. We Were Exploding Anyway is quite special, since it marks a decisive shift in the musical styles of the band. While before this album the band typically produced instrumental Post-Rock with some electronic components, they started moving into a more electronic direction. The first signs of this shift are the audible in the last two songs of We Were Exploding Anyway: Debutante and Tiger Girl. Both songs are decisively electronic, and have a different vibe from their previous releases. I remember the online discussions when this album first dropped, where fans were curious what the future of 65daysofstatic would bring. Unlike what happened when Linkin Park released their “Castle of Glass” single, which was taken very badly by their fan base, 65daysofstatic fans were more curious and open about it.

For me, personally, 65daysofstatic is a very “haptic” band that had a decisively physical influence on me, too. For one, they used to build their own guitar effect pedals and release the schematics. As a child from a very “electrophilic” household, I, too, attempted building one of their pedals for myself — to little success, unfortunately. A few years later, in September 2013, I had another fantastic experience with them. Being affiliated with the university’s radio station, I got hold of them when they stopped for a gig at the Luxor in Cologne. I remember sitting in the coach with them, chit-chatting with them about their music and the upcoming tour, recording an interview for the upcoming shows. Lovely bunch of folks, and I *really* hope that I can meet them again at some point.
