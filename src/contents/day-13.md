This is another of those recommendations that I would probably haven't seen in my inbox if it hadn't been for the algorithmic playlist that started with some artists from the broader ecosystem around Sarah Longfield, Plini, and the likes. At some point, either Spotify or Apple Music (I can't remember) dumped Mr. Invisible into my feed, and I was immediately hooked. The progression, instrumentation, arrangement, and vocals all matched very well and are still, to this day, extremely creative.

You must imagine this album and the sound of Thank You, Scientist, as a quite odd mixture between Coheed and Cambria (vocals and some of the riff choices), Snarky Puppy (the arrangements and the instrumental variety), Protest the Hero (the overall energy), and, yes, even some Ska (although I am not too familiar with that genre, so I couldn't name names) in some stretches. I think I might also appreciate Thank you, Scientist also a little because of their name. But that's just a theory.

Overall, the entire album is a good roundtrip around things I like in my music, but of course reimagined. Mr. Invisible holds a special place, because even after I listened to the entire album several times, that specific song still comes up on top. But as with all my other suggestions, this naturally does not imply that the other songs here are any less great.
