Man, if there is an album that combines the atmosphere of the country of origin of the artist with the collective feeling of Covid and that perfectly describes the overall mood and connects both in a single record, it must be The Quiet Place Away. This is one of the few albums that I accidentally discovered shortly after its release date and which I have listened to in an excessive manner that I only do rarely.

Originally, I discovered I Built the Sky via a random playlist on Spotify that focused on progressive metal from the likes of Plini, Sarah Longfield, and others, which is bound to happen if you listen to Between the Buried and Me. What is nice about The Quiet Place Away is that it’s at the same time very similar to other releases he made, while at the same time very different. Instead of technical and complex guitar-heavy pieces, here we merely hear acoustic guitars and extremely light music.

All in all, it’s a very serene album that is especially relaxing in summer. I have made great experience on long car rides. If you want to listen to what he typically does, give The Sky is Not the Limit a listen, also available on Bandcamp. I recommend especially track #10 — Aviaticus, which is by far my favorite.
