Originally, I discovered Robots with Rayguns via Apple Music, as Wild Style’s “Sweat it Out” was flushed into one of my random playlists. When I switched to my own music library, I was pleasantly surprised to find them on Bandcamp, and proceeded to go shopping a bit.

It’s one of these artists that seem to stem from the huge Vaporwave/Synthwave trend we’ve seen in the 2010s. What I like about it is that Robots with Rayguns’ albums aren’t as slow paced as some of the other, more prominent artists in the scene (just think of the endless covers and remixes of Mariya Takeuchi’s Plastic Love, or James Ferraro’s Far Side Virtual), while maintaining the “digital sunset with pixelated palm trees” atmosphere that sets the genre apart.
