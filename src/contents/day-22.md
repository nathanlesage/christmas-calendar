This is one of the first albums I bought when I entered my hardcore era, initiated by a bunch of friends who were part of a circle of bands in the Bonn area. I have lots of fond memories of these days, and I will have a bunch of other anecdotes to share. But now to the album itself. It took me a few weeks to fully warm up to the style of very rough, unpolished music that is the wider genre of hardcore or emo.

I never fully understood the specific genre divisions in the scene, but regardless of that, the music is typically emotionally on the less bright side, and the songs cover quite dark themes. But after getting used to this almost diametrically opposed style to the music I typically listen to otherwise, I grew quite fond of it. I don’t listen to hardcore all that often, but when I do, it always gives me a feeling of relieve. Stage Four is especially a great record since there are multiple great songs that could’ve easily counted as favorites here. But it’s one of these albums where not one song really stands out, but where all songs rather contribute to a general vibe, a feeling that does something to you.

The longer you listen to the record, and the more you take note of the lyrics, the more you feel appreciative of what you have. It’s not really something you can describe in words, you’ll have to listen. Luckily, listening is free on Bandcamp, so go, give it a try!
