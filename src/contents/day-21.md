These folks likely hold the record for having the longest band name of all bands that I know of. In fact, their name is so long, that they themselves decided to abbreviate it to “The world is” on their Bandcamp page. The band is part of a cosmos of similar bands that have something … odd about them. I can’t really put my finger on it, but bands like The World Is and …And You Will Know Us By The Trail Of Dead have a very particular style that, at least for my brain, just belongs together. Their music is characterized by a rough tone that sounds a bit off; the melodies are just that tiny bit away from a perfect harmony, and in general it gives me a bit the feeling of a half-done building that’s just missing something. You know, Neubauten before they … Einstürzen. … that joke has just been completely obliterated by the peculiarities of German grammar.

It is certainly not an album I would listen to *every week*, but still, it has a nice aura and serves that peculiar mood quite well. In addition, the entire album remains interesting throughout. While except for “No Pilgrim,” no song really stands out as exceptional, as I mentioned in another review, I believe that this is also a sign of quality, as it means that they produce a consistently great sound. And I do believe that is the case. Again, one has to be in a certain mood to enjoy the particular style, but when one is, this is an excellent album to listen to.
