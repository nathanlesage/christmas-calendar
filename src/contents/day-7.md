Do you know this feeling when the summer is hot and dry, and you start to get this melancholic feeling that has become more prevalent now that climate change is really kicking in? That’s what this record does to me. Whenever I listen in, I have this dual feeling of “summer plus depression.” I mean, Sorority Noise is an emo band, and as such one would expect that. But what I do appreciate is that they manage to convey this feeling in such a skilled way. Unlike more hardcore-leaning bands, Sorority Noise achieves this with merely well-selected chord progressions and lyrics.

You’re Not as _____ as You Think is certainly one of those records that you can only bear to listen when you’re in the right mood, but when you are, it is a very powerful valve for certain sets of emotions. And, while you’re listening to it, you can think of various adjectives that you are not as much of as you would think. Which also has something meditative to it.
