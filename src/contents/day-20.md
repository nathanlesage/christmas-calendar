You may have never heard of sungazer, but you may know their bass player: Adam Neely. He’s a very prolific YouTube creator focusing on musical theory and a bunch of related stuff. I used to watch every new video a few years back. I stopped for reasons unbeknownst to me, but I can absolutely recommend his work. He’s very active towards celebrating music as an art form, and has recently started to pick fights with AI generated music, which is a breeze of fresh air.

But anyhow, when he doesn’t do videos, he makes music. And what incredible music sungazer makes. After I have re-established my music library, I watched a video by him, which reminded me of his band, so I checked Bandcamp, and, to nobody’s surprise, they’re available. Against the Fall of Night is a somewhat odd album, because it combines a plethora of musical styles, but in a way that is frankly incomprehensibly good. If you asked me before, I would have laughed at you proposing to combine synthesizers with technical guitar solos, world music, and the odd harmonies of jazz. But, as we all learned by watching Neely’s content, if done correctly, jazz is an entire world of harmonies and possibilities. And this album is a testament to that.

In this case, selecting a favorite song was similarly difficult as with some other albums in this list, but not because they all somehow fulfill the same desires, but because they are *quite literally different genres*. How do you pick a favorite song in an album that contains nine different genres, completely different vibes, but where each individual one hits you like no other? Really, the entire album is a journey through massive plains of musical styles. It’s somewhat like King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard, but instead of following the same genre for one album before moving to another on, they essentially compressed this very efficiently into a single album. But after a difficult decision, I decided to give it to *Oeteldonk*, an odd name for an amazing song. It almost gives you “end credits” vibes, but it’s just a masterpiece through and through.
